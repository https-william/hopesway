Supported Independent Living (SIL)

The biggest worry families have is that moving into SIL means handing over control to an impersonal facility where their loved one is just another room number. At Hopesway, you choose who you live with and keep your own daily rhythms, from when you wake up to what's for dinner, with our staff quietly supporting your independence around the clock.

Who this is for

  • Adults with an NDIS plan who want the freedom and comfort of living semi-independently in a welcoming shared or solo home.
  • Participants transitioning out of the family home who need reliable, 24/7 on-site support with cooking, personal care, and medications.
  • Families seeking long-term security, carefully matched housemates, and genuine stability across Pimpama and Northern Gold Coast.

02

Support Coordination

Most participants feel completely lost trying to decode dense 40-page NDIS plan budgets, terrified of accidentally leaving thousands in therapy funding unspent. We take the confusion and endless paperwork off your plate, tracking down trusted local specialists and fighting to make sure your funding actually delivers what was promised.

Who this is for

  • Families feeling overwhelmed by bureaucratic NDIS portals, complicated line items, and uncooperative service agreements.
  • Participants who want an experienced advocate coordinating their occupational therapists, speech pathologists, and daily support teams.
  • Anyone approaching an upcoming NDIS plan review who wants airtight progress reports and evidence prepared to safeguard their funding.

03
Registered Category: Short Term Accommodation (STA) · Specialised Disability Accommodation (SDA)

Short Term Accommodation & Respite

Loving carers often feel intense guilt even thinking about taking a weekend off, wondering if anyone else will understand their family member's unique routines and emotional triggers. Our respite stays feel like a genuine, refreshing holiday with engaging activities and home-cooked meals, giving primary carers total peace of mind to recharge.

Who this is for

  • Dedicated primary carers needing planned or short-notice relief to rest, recover from illness, or attend to personal commitments.
  • Participants eager for an enjoyable weekend break away from home to practice practical independence, make new friends, and explore the coast.
  • Families needing emergency interim support during sudden hospital discharge or urgent home modifications.

Registered Category: Development-Life Skills · Community Participation · Therapeutic Supports

Daily Life & Community Participation

Too many programs promise 'community access' only to leave participants sitting in sterile group centres watching television all morning. We focus entirely on what you genuinely love doing, whether that's hitting the gym, taking cooking classes, catching a train to Brisbane, or volunteering at an animal sanctuary.

Who this is for

  • Participants wanting to build practical confidence with everyday tasks like grocery budgeting, meal planning, and public transport navigation.
  • Young adults seeking an active, relatable support buddy who shares their hobbies, gaming interests, artistic passions, or sporting goals.
  • Anyone experiencing social isolation who wants a supportive, encouraging push to participate meaningfully in local Gold Coast community life.

05
Registered Category: Assist-Travel / Community Transport

Transport Support

Relying on taxis or rideshares often means stressful last-minute cancellations, missed specialist appointments, and drivers who don't understand mobility aids or sensory needs. Our dedicated drivers arrive early in clean, accessible vehicles, walking you right to the reception desk so getting from A to B is completely stress-free.

Who this is for

  • Participants who cannot drive or find public buses and trains overwhelming, inaccessible, or unpredictable.
  • Individuals needing reliable, scheduled transport to therapy clinics, hospital outpatient appointments, university lectures, or workplace shifts.
  • Families who need peace of mind knowing their loved one is safely accompanied door-to-door by CPR-trained, background-checked staff.

06
Registered Categories: Household Tasks · Community Nursing Care · CHSP & HCP

Aged Care Support (CHSP / Home Care Packages)

Aging parents dread the thought of losing their independence and being forced into residential aged care before they are ready. We provide a gentle, respectful helping hand around the house: help with morning routines, warm home-cooked meals, and garden maintenance, so you can stay safely where you feel comfortable.

Who this is for

  • Older Australians wanting to live safely, independently, and happily in their own family home for as long as possible.
  • Seniors holding Commonwealth Home Support Programme (CHSP) or Home Care Packages needing flexible, trusted domestic and personal care.
  • Adult children looking for dependable, compassionate carers who treat their aging mum or dad like cherished extended family.
