Frequently Asked Questions About Travel

Transparent, reassuring answers on how our support workers travel to you under your NDIS or Home Care package.

Yes, absolutely. Every support shift begins wherever is most comfortable and convenient for you, whether that is your family home, independent unit, workplace, educational institution, local pool, or community club. You never have to worry about commuting to a sterile office to receive support.
We bill provider travel strictly in accordance with the official NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. For our established service clusters across Gold Coast, Logan, Townsville, and surrounds, travel time is claimed from your plan's Core (Category 04) or Capacity Building (Category 15) funding, capped at NDIS standard limits. There are never any unexpected out-of-pocket surcharges.
Yes. In addition to in-home care, our workers operate clean, fully insured vehicles (including wheelchair-accessible vans) to provide door-to-door transport. We accompany you right into hospital outpatient clinics, therapy appointments, or grocery stores, ensuring you feel safe, unhurried, and respected throughout the outing.
We frequently coordinate care for participants living in rural communities surrounding Charters Towers, Ingham, Ayr, and the Scenic Rim / Jimboomba corridor. We group appointments and schedule local support workers to minimize your plan travel costs. Simply give our intake desk a call at 1300 694 673 and we'll check our route availability for your address.
